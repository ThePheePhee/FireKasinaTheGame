# Resource and editorial review

Review date: **13 September 2026**. Scope: the isolated `codex/retreat-review` branch; no publication is implied by this document.

## What was checked

All user-facing data copy in `src/data` was read: main locations, named roads, discoveries, walkable rooms, NPCs, and all five Quarrelsome Inn conversation graphs. The old `subMaps.ts` card-map prototype and existing project documentation were also inspected. The live Game and Sandbox both use `interiorMaps.ts`; the old cards are not imported by the application. They are now explicitly marked historical so that their shorter, outdated descriptions are not mistaken for the current curriculum.

Every distinct teaching/community URL exported by these data files was submitted to the web reader. For accessible pages, the relevant text was read—not merely an HTTP status. A successful read establishes what that author or tradition says, not the truth of every practice or supernatural claim. This was not a clinical evaluation, an archive-wide review, a Discord membership check, or a claim that every page nested under a linked homepage was read. Publication and design-reference URLs are listed separately below.

Status meanings: **Read** = relevant page content retrieved and inspected; **Partial** = resource identified but the needed material could not all be examined; **Unverified** = the tool could not retrieve the target. Tool restrictions are not evidence that a link is broken for a human visitor. Search-indexed excerpts are not silently treated as a successful visit.

## Editorial decisions and preserved voice

- The sources range from a canonical text to a modern manual, personal diaries, and community discussion. Their status is explicit without stripping wonder out of the fiction. No new author quotation is invented.
- The early candle/dot cycle and meters are identified as Game mechanics. The homestead NPC explains Sandbox’s open gates; gameplay numbers are not real-world attainments.
- The Library no longer calls every book a trustworthy map. The reader gets different types of source rather than a blanket endorsement.
- The Mists retain their relationship to the Murk. Boohoo Lagoon does not call sadness a failed meditation. Healing practices retain their four requested identities while separating consent, care, tradition, and efficacy claims.
- Molten Gold still courts and dances; the Plastic remains mercurial; Scale Sheets remain delightfully oversized; Darth Vader has not been evicted. The Inn still acknowledges that useful arguments helped create this game.
- The canonical second jhāna is a spring-fed **lake**, not another lotus pond. Existing relevant art and descriptions are preserved rather than repeating an earlier loose paraphrase from the conversation.
- The four awakening rooms keep traditional distinctions without implying that ordinary questioning ends at stream entry or that this game evaluates anyone’s attainment. Divine visitors remain alongside the four brahmavihāra gardens, with accounts distinguished from verified external facts.
- All five conversation trees were read, including deliberately foolish choices and failures. Mistress Metaphor no longer drops an unintroduced puppet into her fish story. Brother Anecdote no longer turns “causality is uncertain” into “the tea definitely did not cause it.” His allergy branch now stops serving first; his inquiry branch records events without instructing a repeat herbal experiment. Destinations, hidden-trait gates, effects, and authored endings are unchanged.

The existing exhaustive conversation tests check every non-repeating route for structural completion and bounded effects; that does not prove literary quality. The editorial pass additionally followed the correction/recovery, evasive, symbolic-but-harmless, unsafe-escalation, and public-repair strands described in `inn-review.md`. New content tests protect live source coverage, requested curriculum, source labels, and the exact supplied links. The status of blocked resources remains visible in this ledger rather than being converted into a passing network test.

## Deliberately unresolved

1. Human/browser confirmation of the supplied Discord channel and Second Screen demo; no replacement or invented invitation was made.
2. Full PDF and audio-content review where extraction/playback was unavailable. Nearby claims now use accessible evidence or a neutral book label.
3. Independent evidence for supernatural/medical claims is outside this editorial audit. The prose reports traditions and experience accounts without representing those claims as established facts.
4. The historical card-map data is retained, clearly deprecated, rather than deleted or silently allowed to diverge into a second live teaching map.
